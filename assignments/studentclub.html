Tech Connect Student Club

Welcome to Tech Connect! We are a group of students who enjoy technology and want to learn together. Join us for workshops, coding challenges, and team projects.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help students learn about technology, get hands-on experience, and meet others with similar interests. Everyone is welcome, even if you are just getting started.

Benefits of Membership

  • Learn new skills through workshops and seminars.
  • Participate in hackathons and coding competitions.
  • Meet industry professionals and alumni.
  • Work with other students on technology projects.
  • Get help and advice from experienced members.

Upcoming Activities

Activities for Members

  • Web development workshops to practice HTML and CSS
  • Monthly coding challenges to build problem-solving skills
  • Guest speakers who share their technology career experiences
  • Team hackathons to plan and build projects
  • Computer hardware workshops to learn about computer parts

Dates, times, and locations will be announced.

Technology Resources for Members

These free resources can help you practice outside of club meetings.

  • MDN Web Docs — Learn the basics of HTML and CSS.
  • freeCodeCamp — Practice coding with lessons and projects.

How to Join

  1. Check for an announcement about the next club meeting.
  2. Attend an orientation to learn more about the club.
  3. Choose an activity or project and get involved.

Official meeting and membership details will be added when available.
